Starbucks' Strategic Store Location Selection

Unveiling the data-driven approach to precise decision-making in store expansion.

Global Coffee Market and Starbucks' Strategy

The global coffee market is growing steadily, with an average annual growth rate of 10%. Starbucks faces fierce competition from various brands. Precise store location selection is crucial for its expansion strategy.

Data Collection

Starbucks collects diverse data, including GIS data for geographical insights, consumer behavior data from online and offline sources, competitor data from business platforms, and government demographic data to understand the target consumer group.

Data Analysis Technologies

Advanced techniques such as the DBSCAN algorithm for spatial clustering, the random forest model for machine learning prediction, and confidence interval verification are employed to analyze data and uncover key factors for location selection.

Location Selection Model Based on PACE Framework

The PACE framework (Plan, Analyze, Construct, Execute) provides a systematic approach. Data preprocessing, feature engineering, model integration, and optimization are carried out to build an effective location selection model.

Coping with Complex Scenarios and Challenges

Starbucks has strategies to deal with emerging business districts, dynamic market changes, special events like the pandemic, and long-term external factors through data-driven adjustments and early planning.

Implementation and Follow-up Evaluation of Location Selection Decisions

A/B testing and dynamic monitoring are used to evaluate the effectiveness of location selection decisions. A comprehensive evaluation index system is established to ensure continuous optimization.
